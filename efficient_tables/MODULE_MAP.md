## Added after the reorganization (no old flat name)

| new | what it is |
| --- | --- |
| `Foundations.SmallPoint.{Defs,CofactorBound,ExtremeDecomposition,ConicCaratheodory,Main}` | small integer point in a polyhedron, `(t+1)·t!·B^t` |
| `Foundations.RuntimeComposition.{Defs,Cutoff,Factorial,TripleCount,LocalFactor,Main}` | arithmetic composition of stage-wise cost bounds |
| `Foundations.SeparationLemmas.{Defs,DefinitionCheck,Peeling,ZeroCriterion,Integrality,Main}` | zero and anchored-integrality criteria for signed sums of powers of two |
| `Foundations.PairedCharge.{Defs,PointList,ChargeClasses,SkeletonUnfold,ChargeFormula,Bound,Main}` | charge counting of the paired constructor's skeleton |
| `Provider.Ceiling.GroupedCharge` | exact charge of `groupedLiteralBaseline` |

The theorem names inside these files are not prefixed by the layer: for example `SmallPoint.small_integer_point`, `RuntimeComposition.composition`, `SeparationLemmas.zero_criterion`, `PairedCharge.charge_le` and `Paper.F3ChargeCeiling.groupedLiteralBaseline_charge`.
